# Mission and Context

**Google Developer Groups on Campus (GDGoC) Holy Angel University** is a student-led technology community powered by Google Developers. It is part of a global network aimed at helping students learn, build, and collaborate on real-world technology projects.

## Our Mission

To empower students with technical and professional skills by providing opportunities for hands-on learning, collaboration, and real-world problem-solving in technology.

## Our Vision

To build a strong and inclusive community of future developers and innovators who can contribute meaningfully to the global tech ecosystem.

## Why We Built Axis

The GDG HAU Axis platform was created to centralize and automate the operations of the organization. As the community grew, managing members, event attendance, and credentialing (certificates and ID cards) manually became unsustainable.

Axis serves as the digital backbone of GDG HAU, providing:

- **Member Identity:** A central, verified digital identity (GDG ID) for every member.
- **Event Management:** Streamlined event registration, ticketing, and attendance tracking.
- **Gamification & Rewards:** A points ledger system where members earn points for participation and can redeem them in a marketplace.
- **Credentials:** Automated generation and verification of event certificates and digital badges.

## Technology Focus Areas

The GDG HAU community emphasizes learning in various tech domains, which the platform itself reflects in its architecture:

- Software Engineering & Web Development
- Mobile Application Development
- Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
- Cloud Computing & DevOps
- API Development & UI/UX Design

By contributing to the Axis platform, student developers gain hands-on experience building production-grade systems using modern workflows, preparing them for the software industry.
